When programming, you must turn on the power switch of the base. Turning the power switch to the left turns it on, and turning it to the right turns it off. Type-C is used for programming, and the USB port is selected for programming the ESP32-S31. Type-C is used for data transmission and HID function when inserted reversely. After programming, power it on again.
(Type-C has a metal packaging port. When the packaging port is facing downwards, it is called "right insertion"; when it is facing upwards, it is called "reverse insertion")
