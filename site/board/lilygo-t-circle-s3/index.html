T-Circle S3

LilyGO · LilyGO T-Circle S3 – ESP32-S3 0.75" 160×160 round TFT with speaker + mic

T-Circle S3 is a 0.75" 160x160 TFT ESP32 display board by LilyGO based on the ESP32-S3 with 16MB flash, 8MB PSRAM.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • SPI display
  • 8 MB PSRAM
  • 16 MB flash
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 5 (LE) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Speaker
  • Microphone
  • Battery charger: USB charging 5 V 500 mA
  • RGB LED: Three-colour LED
