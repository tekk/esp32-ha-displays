ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.28

Waveshare · Waveshare ESP32-S3 1.28" 240×240 round touch board

ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.28 is an 1.28" 240x240 Capacitive touch ESP32 display board by Waveshare based on the ESP32-S3 with 16MB flash, 2MB PSRAM.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • Capacitive touch
  • 2 MB PSRAM
  • 16 MB flash
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 5 (LE) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • USB-C
  • GPIO header 6x GPIO via SH1.0 connector
  • Battery: 3.7 V MX1.25 lithium battery header
  • IMU: QMI8658 6-axis
