CrowPanel 2.1" Rotary

Elecrow · Elecrow CrowPanel 2.1" HMI rotary knob – ESP32-S3 480×480 round IPS touch

CrowPanel 2.1" Rotary is a 2.1" 480x480 IPS Capacitive touch ESP32 display board by Elecrow based on the ESP32-S3 with 16MB flash, 8MB PSRAM.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • Capacitive touch
  • 8 MB PSRAM
  • 16 MB flash
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 5 (LE) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Serial port (UART): 1x UART1, 1x UART0 (ZX-MX 1.25-4P)
  • I2C (ZX-MX 1.25-4P)
  • Rotary knob with press switch
  • Buttons: RESET, BOOT, knob press
  • Enclosure / shell
