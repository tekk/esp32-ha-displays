CoreInk

M5Stack · M5Stack CoreInk – ESP32-PICO 1.54" 200×200 e-ink

CoreInk is an 1.54" 200x200 E-paper ESP32 display board by M5Stack based on the ESP32 with 4MB flash.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • SPI display
  • E-paper display (GDEY0154D67, black/white)
  • 4 MB flash
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 4.2 BR/EDR (Classic) + BLE – version from the ESP32 datasheet
  • USB-C
  • Grove port: HY2.0-4P
  • Buzzer: Passive buzzer
  • RTC: BM8563
  • Buttons: 3-way dial switch, user, reset, power
