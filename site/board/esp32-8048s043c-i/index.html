ESP32-8048S043C_I

Guition · 4.3 inch ESP32P4 display module

ESP32-8048S043C_I is a 4.3" 800x480 IPS Capacitive touch ESP32 display board by Guition based on the ESP32-S3 with 16MB flash, 8MB PSRAM.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • Capacitive touch
  • 8 MB PSRAM
  • 16 MB flash
  • microSD (TF) card slot
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 5 (LE) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • GPIO / IO port expansion
