Paper Comm Edition

M5Stack · M5Stack Paper Comm Edition

Paper Comm Edition is a 4.7" 540x960 E-Ink Capacitive touch ESP32 display board by M5Stack based on the ESP32 with 16MB flash, 8MB PSRAM.
