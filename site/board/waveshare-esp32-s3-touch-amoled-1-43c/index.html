ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.43C

Waveshare · ESP32-S3 1.43inch AMOLED Round Touch Display Development Board, 466 × 466 resolution, Supports AI speech interaction and online LLM platforms, dual-core process

ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.43C is an 1.43" 466x466 AMOLED Capacitive touch ESP32 display board by Waveshare based on the ESP32-S3 with 8MB flash, 8MB PSRAM.

  • QSPI display
  • Capacitive touch (CST820)
  • 8 MB PSRAM
  • 8 MB flash
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 5 (LE) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
