ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.83

Waveshare · ESP32-S3 1.83inch Touch Display Development Board, 240 × 284, Onboard Audio Codec Chip, Built-in Microphones And Speaker, Supports Wi-Fi And BLE 5 | ESP32-S3-To

ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.83 is an 1.83" 240x284 IPS Capacitive touch ESP32 display board by Waveshare based on the ESP32-S3 with 16MB flash, 8MB PSRAM.

Features, ports & interfaces

  • SPI display
  • Capacitive touch (CST816D)
  • 8 MB PSRAM
  • 16 MB flash
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 5 (LE) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • GPIO / IO port expansion
  • Speaker: MX1.25 speaker header
  • Microphone: Onboard dual microphones array
  • Battery: 1.2mm Lithium battery header
  • RTC: PCF85063 – RTC chip
  • IMU: QMI8658 – 6-axis IMU includes a 3-axis gyroscope and a 3-axis accelerometer
