MaTouch ESP32-S3 Parallel TFT 3.5" ILI9488

Makerfabs · Makerfabs MaTouch ESP32-S3 Parallel TFT with Touch 3.5" (ILI9488, 16-bit parallel)

MaTouch ESP32-S3 Parallel TFT 3.5" ILI9488 is a 3.5" 480x320 TFT Capacitive touch ESP32 display board by Makerfabs based on the ESP32-S3 with 16MB flash, 8MB PSRAM.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • 8080 parallel (MCU) display
  • Capacitive touch (FT6236)
  • 8 MB PSRAM
  • 16 MB flash
  • microSD (TF) card slot
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 5 (LE) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • USB-C (USB-to-UART)
  • Grove port: Mabee: 1x I2C, 1x GPIO
  • Buttons: Flash + reset
