T-Panel S3

LilyGO · LilyGO T-Panel S3 – ESP32-S3 + ESP32-H2 4" 480×480 RGB panel (RS-485 / CAN option)

T-Panel S3 is a 4.0" 480x480 IPS ESP32 display board by LilyGO based on the ESP32-S3 with 16MB flash, 8MB PSRAM.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • RGB parallel display
  • 8 MB PSRAM
  • 16 MB flash
  • microSD (TF) card slot
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 5 (LE) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Thread (802.15.4) – IEEE 802.15.4 radio of the ESP32-H2 co-processor – SoC capability per the Espressif datasheet; needs firmware support
  • Zigbee (802.15.4) – IEEE 802.15.4 radio of the ESP32-H2 co-processor – SoC capability per the Espressif datasheet; needs firmware support
  • Matter-capable radio (Wi-Fi / Thread) – ESP32-H2 co-processor capability per the Espressif datasheet; firmware-dependent
  • RS-485
  • CAN FD (optional module)
  • Grove port: 2x Qwiic
  • Buttons: 4x (S3 Boot + RST, H2 Boot + RST)
