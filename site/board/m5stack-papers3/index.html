PaperS3

M5Stack · M5Stack PaperS3 – ESP32-S3 4.7" 960×540 touch e-paper

PaperS3 is a 4.7" 960x540 E-paper Capacitive touch ESP32 display board by M5Stack based on the ESP32-S3 with 16MB flash, 8MB Octal PSRAM.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • E-paper display (ET047TC1, 16-level grayscale)
  • Capacitive touch (GT911)
  • 8 MB PSRAM – Octal
  • 16 MB flash
  • microSD (TF) card slot
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 5 (LE) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • USB-C (USB OTG)
  • GPIO header HC1.25-4P (3V3, GND, 2x GPIO)
  • Buzzer: Passive buzzer
  • Battery: 1800 mAh @ 3.7 V (MX1.25-2P)
  • Battery charger: LGS4056H
  • RTC: BM8563
  • IMU: BMI270
