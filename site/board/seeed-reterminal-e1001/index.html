reTerminal E1001

Seeed Studio · Seeed reTerminal E1001 – 7.5" Monochrome (4-level grayscale) ESP32-S3 e-paper terminal

reTerminal E1001 is a 7.5" 800x480 E-paper ESP32 display board by Seeed Studio based on the ESP32-S3 with 32MB flash, 8MB PSRAM.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • E-paper display (Monochrome (4-level grayscale) (GDEY075T7 panel, UC8179 controller))
  • 8 MB PSRAM
  • 32 MB flash
  • microSD (TF) card slot – up to 32 GB, FAT32
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 5 (LE) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • USB-C
  • GPIO / IO port expansion
  • Microphone
  • Buzzer (sound alert)
  • Battery: 2000 mAh (~3 months)
  • Metal enclosure
