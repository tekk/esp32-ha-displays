ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-2.8B

Waveshare · ESP32-S3 2.8inch Display Development Board Type B, 480×640, IPS, 32-bit LX7 Dual-core Processor, Up To 240MHz Frequency, Supports WiFi & Bluetooth, ESP32 With D

ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-2.8B is a 2.8" 480x640 IPS LCD ESP32 display board by Waveshare based on the ESP32-S3 with 16MB flash, 8MB PSRAM.

Features, ports & interfaces

  • RGB parallel display
  • 8 MB PSRAM
  • 16 MB flash
  • microSD (TF) card slot
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 5 (LE) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • GPIO / IO port expansion
  • Buzzer
  • Battery: RTC battery header
  • RTC chip
  • IMU: QMI8658 (6-axis IMU includes a 3-axis gyroscope and a 3-axis accelerometer)
