T-RGB

LilyGO · LilyGO T-RGB – ESP32-S3 2.1"/2.8" round 480×480 RGB IPS touch

T-RGB is a 2.1" / 2.8" (versions) 480x480 IPS Capacitive touch ESP32 display board by LilyGO based on the ESP32-S3 with 8MB (in-package, ESP32-S3R8) PSRAM.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • RGB parallel display
  • Capacitive touch (FT3267)
  • 8 MB PSRAM (in package)
  • microSD (TF) card slot
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 5 (LE) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Battery voltage detection (IO04)
  • Buttons: Boot, Reset
