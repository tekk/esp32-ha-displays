ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-2.8

Waveshare · Waveshare ESP32-S3 2.8" 240×320 touch dev board (IMU, RTC, speaker)

ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-2.8 is a 2.8" 240x320 Capacitive touch ESP32 display board by Waveshare based on the ESP32-S3 with 16MB flash, 8MB PSRAM.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • Capacitive touch (optional)
  • 8 MB PSRAM
  • 16 MB flash
  • microSD (TF) card slot
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 5 (LE) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Speaker: Onboard speaker
  • Battery recharge management
  • RTC
  • IMU: QMI8658 6-axis
