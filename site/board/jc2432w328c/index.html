JC2432W328C

Guition · ESP32 display lcd

JC2432W328C is a 2.8" 320x240 TN Capacitive touch ESP32 display board by Guition based on the ESP32 with 4MB flash, - PSRAM.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • SPI display
  • Capacitive touch
  • 4 MB flash
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 4.2 BR/EDR (Classic) + BLE – version from the ESP32 datasheet
  • Serial port (UART)
  • Speaker interface
