PaperMono-Lite

M5Stack · M5Stack PaperMono-Lite

PaperMono-Lite is a 3.97" 480x800 E-Ink ESP32 display board by M5Stack based on the ESP32-S3 with 16MB flash, 8MB Octal PSRAM.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • E-paper display
  • 8 MB PSRAM – Octal
  • 16 MB flash
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Microphone: PDM microphone LMD4737T261-AC02
  • Battery: 1150mAh
  • RTC: RX8130CE
  • IMU: BMI270
