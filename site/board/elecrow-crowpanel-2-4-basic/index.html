CrowPanel 2.4" Basic

Elecrow · Elecrow CrowPanel ESP32 2.4" HMI – ESP32 240×320 SPI resistive

CrowPanel 2.4" Basic is a 2.4" 240x320 TN Resistive touch ESP32 display board by Elecrow based on the ESP32 with 4MB flash.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • SPI display
  • Resistive touch
  • 4 MB flash
  • microSD (TF) card slot
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 4.2 BR/EDR (Classic) + BLE – version from the ESP32 datasheet
  • Serial port (UART): UART (HY2.0-4P)
  • I2C (HY2.0-4P)
  • GPIO header GPIO_D (HY2.0-4P)
  • Speaker connector (8 Ω 2 W recommended)
  • Battery connector PH2.0-2P, 3.7–4.2 V
  • Buttons: BOOT + Reset
