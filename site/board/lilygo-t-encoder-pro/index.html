T-Encoder Pro

LilyGO · LilyGO T-Encoder Pro – ESP32-S3 1.2" 390×390 round AMOLED knob

T-Encoder Pro is an 1.2" 390x390 AMOLED ESP32 display board by LilyGO based on the ESP32-S3 with 16MB flash, 8MB PSRAM.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • QSPI display
  • 8 MB PSRAM
  • 16 MB flash
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 5 (LE) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Grove port: 2x Qwiic 4-pin
  • Buzzer (IO17)
  • Rotary encoder
