JC8048Q350N_I

Guition · Industrial hmi panel

JC8048Q350N_I is a 5" 800x480 IPS ESP32 display board by Guition based on the ArtInChip D12x with 16MB flash, 8MB PSRAM.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • RGB parallel display
  • 8 MB PSRAM
  • 16 MB flash
  • Serial (UART) – MX1.25 4-pin connector
  • Speaker – MX1.25 2-pin connector (≤ 3 W)
