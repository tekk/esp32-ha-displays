CoreP4X

M5Stack · M5Stack CoreP4X

CoreP4X is a 2.64" 480x480 ESP32 display board by M5Stack based on the ESP32-P4 with 16MB flash, 32MB PSRAM.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • 32 MB PSRAM
  • 16 MB flash
  • microSD (TF) card slot
  • Wi-Fi
  • 2× USB-C (USB OTG)
  • Ethernet RJ45 100 Mbps
  • Speaker: AW8737A amplifier, 8Ω@1W speaker
  • Microphone: ES7210 audio ADC, dual MEMS microphones, AEC support
  • RTC: RX8130CE
  • IMU: LSM6DS3TR-C 6-axis motion sensor
