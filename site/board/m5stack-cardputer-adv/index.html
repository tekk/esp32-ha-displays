Cardputer-Adv

M5Stack · M5Stack Cardputer-Adv – ESP32-S3 card computer, ES8311 audio, IMU

Cardputer-Adv is an 1.14" 240x135 TFT ESP32 display board by M5Stack based on the ESP32-S3 with 8MB flash.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • SPI display
  • 8 MB flash
  • microSD (TF) card slot
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 5 (LE) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • IR emitter
  • Grove port: HY2.0-4P + EXT 2.54-14P
  • Speaker: 8 Ω 1 W, NS4150B
  • Microphone: MEMS microphone (65 dB SNR)
  • Battery: 1500 mAh
  • IMU: BMI270
  • Keyboard: 56 keys (4 x 14)
