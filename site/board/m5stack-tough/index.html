Tough

M5Stack · M5Stack Tough – industrial ESP32 2.0" touch controller (IP-rated case)

Tough is a 2.0" 320x240 IPS Capacitive touch ESP32 display board by M5Stack based on the ESP32 with 16MB flash, 8MB Quad PSRAM.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • SPI display
  • Capacitive touch (CHSC6540)
  • 8 MB PSRAM – Quad
  • 16 MB flash
  • microSD (TF) card slot
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 4.2 BR/EDR (Classic) + BLE – version from the ESP32 datasheet
  • USB-C (USB-to-UART)
  • RS-485
  • GPIO / IO port expansion
  • Speaker: 1 W, NS4168 I2S amplifier
  • DC input: 6–24 V via RS-485 port
  • RTC: BM8563
  • Enclosure / shell
