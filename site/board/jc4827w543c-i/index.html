JC4827W543C_I

Guition · HMI Display Module

JC4827W543C_I is a 4.3" 480x272 IPS Resistive touch ESP32 display board by Guition based on the ESP32-S3 with 4MB flash, 8MB PSRAM.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • QSPI display
  • Resistive touch
  • 8 MB PSRAM
  • 4 MB flash
  • microSD (TF) card slot
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 5 (LE) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • GPIO expansion (up to 10 I/O)
  • Speaker interface
