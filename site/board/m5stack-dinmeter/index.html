DinMeter

M5Stack · M5Stack DinMeter – 1/32 DIN ESP32-S3 1.14" display with encoder

DinMeter is an 1.14" 135x240 TFT ESP32 display board by M5Stack based on the ESP32-S3 with 8MB flash.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • SPI display
  • 8 MB flash
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 5 (LE) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • USB-C (USB OTG, USB-Serial/JTAG)
  • Grove port: 2x Grove (PORT.A, PORT.B)
  • Buzzer: Onboard buzzer
  • Battery: MX1.25-2P battery socket
  • Battery charger: 100 mA charging
  • DC input: Wide input 6–36 V
  • RTC circuit
  • Rotary encoder with button
