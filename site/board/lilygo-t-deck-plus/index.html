T-Deck Plus

LilyGO · LilyGO T-Deck Plus – ESP32-S3 2.8" keyboard device with LoRa + GPS

T-Deck Plus is a 2.8" 320x240 IPS ESP32 display board by LilyGO based on the ESP32-S3 with 16MB flash, 8MB PSRAM.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • SPI display
  • 8 MB PSRAM
  • 16 MB flash
  • microSD (TF) card slot
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 5 (LE) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • LoRa: SX1262, +22 dBm, 433/868/915 MHz
  • GNSS / GPS: GPS
  • Speaker
  • Microphone
  • Keyboard + trackball
