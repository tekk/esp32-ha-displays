JC3636K718C_I(BU)

Guition

JC3636K718C_I(BU) is an 1.85" 360x360 IPS Capacitive touch ESP32 display board by Guition based on the ESP32-S3 with 16MB flash, 8MB PSRAM.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • QSPI display
  • Capacitive touch
  • 8 MB PSRAM
  • 16 MB flash
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 5 (LE) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Speaker interface
  • Enclosure / shell
