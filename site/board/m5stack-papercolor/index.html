PaperColor

M5Stack · M5Stack PaperColor

PaperColor is a 4" 400x600 E-Paper ESP32 display board by M5Stack based on the ESP32-S3 with 16MB flash, 8MB Octal PSRAM.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • E-paper display
  • 8 MB PSRAM – Octal
  • 16 MB flash
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Speaker: 1W @ 8Ω 2520 Speaker, AW8737A Audio Amplifier
  • Microphone: MEMS Microphone, ES7210 Audio ADC, Integrated AEC Circuit
  • Battery: 1250mAh
  • RTC: RX8130CE
