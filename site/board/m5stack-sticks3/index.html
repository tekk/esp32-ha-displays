StickS3

M5Stack · M5Stack StickS3 – ESP32-S3-PICO 1.14" stick with ES8311 audio

StickS3 is an 1.14" 135x240 LCD ESP32 display board by M5Stack based on the ESP32-S3 with 8MB flash, 8MB Octal PSRAM.

Open the interactive page (gallery, files, archives) →

Features, ports & interfaces

  • SPI display
  • 8 MB PSRAM – Octal
  • 8 MB flash
  • Wi-Fi 4 (802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • Bluetooth 5 (LE) – version from the ESP32-S3 datasheet
  • USB-C
  • Grove (HY2.0-4P)
  • Speaker: 8 Ω 1 W, AW8737
  • Microphone: MEMS microphone (65 dB SNR)
  • Battery: 250 mAh
  • IMU: BMI270
